About Brew & Bean

Founded in 2020, Brew & Bean has been serving the community with passion and dedication. We source our coffee beans from sustainable farms around the world and roast them to perfection in-house.

Our mission is to create a welcoming space where people can enjoy exceptional coffee, delicious food, and meaningful connections.
